# Labeling guide: frames that should not be discarded (the cue-labels round)

## Purpose

The sample folder holds 900 copies of frames drawn from 19 of your folders,
none of them a frame you labeled in an earlier round. The question for each
frame is the same as in the discard rounds, **"OK to discard?"**: if Handpic
hid this frame from what you pick from, would that be fine? It is not "is
this frame bad", and not "would you pick it". The labels show how often the
frames that Handpic's cues (the head pose, closed eyes and the rest) single
out are frames you would discard.

## What to mark

**Mark a frame when it should not be discarded:**

- you might pick it;
- you would want to see it before deciding;
- you would mind if Handpic had hidden it from what you pick from.

**Leave it unmarked when you would not miss it**, for example:

- a blurred frame, or one with the eyes closed;
- a dead frame of a burst you would never choose;
- a duplicate you would not look at twice.

When unsure, **mark it (keep)**.

## What the folder holds

The folder mixes several kinds of frames, and nothing shows which is which:
not the name, not the order, not the folder. Judge each frame on its own.

## Setup

1. Open the folder `D:\Photos\tests\2026-11-28-cue-labels\sample`.
2. In the strip header, set **Sort** to **File name** and clear every
   **Filter**. Do this first: both menus are in the strip header.
3. Let the scan finish before you start.
4. Turn the `f` overlay off (press `f` if the AF mark or the face boxes
   show).
5. **Hide the strip and the meta pane**: `ctrl+alt+arrowdown` hides the
   strip, `ctrl+alt+arrowright` the meta pane (on macOS `alt+meta+arrowdown`
   and `alt+meta+arrowright`; `tab` hides both side panes at once). The
   strip's Low and closed-eyes marks and the meta pane's Analysis rows are
   the cues this round measures, so keep them out of sight. The rating and
   the arrow keys work with both hidden.

The files are named `<nnnn>_<folder>_<file>` and shuffled, so the file-name
order is a random order. No frame has a rating, a flag or a label yet.

## Keys

- **`1`** (rating 1) marks the current frame as **keep**. With
  **Auto-advance** on (the settings' Culling page), it moves to the next
  frame; otherwise move with the arrow keys. Either is fine.
- **`0`** clears the rating, to take a mark back.
- **Do not use**: `p` (Pick), `x` / `shift+x` (Reject), any other star
  rating (`2` to `5`) or a color label.

## How to judge

- **Look at the picture.** Do not turn the overlays (`f`, `e`, `d`) on and
  do not bring the strip or the meta pane back while labeling.
- Spend about 3 seconds per frame; 900 frames take about 45 minutes.
- Use as many sittings as you like. Nothing needs saving: each rating is
  written as you press it, and Handpic reopens the folder at the last file
  you viewed (hide the strip and the meta pane again if they come back).
- Do not compare with how you labeled the earlier rounds; judge each frame
  as it is.

## Stopping rule

View **every** frame once, from `0001` to `0900`. A frame left unmarked
counts as OK to discard, so skip none. When unsure, mark it.

## When you are done

Say **"done"**, plus roughly how long it took, and anything odd: frames that
would not open, frames you could not judge, doubts about the definition, and
whether the strip and the meta pane stayed hidden throughout.
