### Findings

1. **[crates/app/ui/src/main.ts:430]**: `scanPass()` reports pass 2 during the prepare phase of a resync. `startScan` calls `setScanRunning(true)` before `scan_folder` resolves, and on the keep-state `resync` path neither `scanId` nor `scanDone` is cleared (only the reset path at line 4531 clears them). So after any finished scan, `scanDone?.scanId === scanId` is still true from the previous scan, and a page turn during `scan_folder` logs `scan=2` although no pass 2 is running; the plan defines this as pass 1 ("pass 1 while `scanRunning`"). Step 2 splits its scenarios by this field, so the mislabel puts folder-listing and reconcile time into the pass-2 numbers. Key the check on something `startScan` changes synchronously: for example, record `currentScan` at `scan-done` (`passTwoOf = currentScan`) and test `passTwoOf === currentScan`.

2. **[crates/app/ui/src/main.ts:3789-3792]**: the handler's work between `receivedAt` and `drawStartedAt` belongs to no stage. That span holds `shown?.bitmap.close()` and `setStatus()`, and `setStatus()` runs a synchronous `renderMeta()`, a DOM rebuild of the meta pane on every switch. `worker` ends at `receivedAt` and `draw` starts at `drawStartedAt`, so the seven stages no longer sum to `keypressToPixels`, which the plan states as the identity ("`keypressToPixels` = the sum"). Two consequences follow: the stacked bar is shorter than the `ms` that `docs/humans/usage.md` says is its height, and main-thread work a page turn pays for is invisible to the R2 / R4 decision rules and to the R5 native-rendering sum. Either take `drawStartedAt` at the top of the handler's success path, so `draw` covers this work (and say so in the plan's stage table and the docs), or make `draw()` run first if the order does not matter. Add a test or a comment pinning that the stages cover the whole span.

### Addressed

1. Fixed: `scanPass()` now tests `passTwoOf === currentScan`, where `passTwoOf` is set from `currentScan` at `scan-done` and `currentScan` is bumped synchronously by `startScan`.
2. Fixed: `drawStartedAt` is taken at the top of the success path, so `draw` covers the bitmap swap and `setStatus()`. The plan's stage table and `docs/humans/performance.md` / `.ja.md` say so; `perf.test.ts` pins that the stages sum to `drawnAt - startedAt`.
