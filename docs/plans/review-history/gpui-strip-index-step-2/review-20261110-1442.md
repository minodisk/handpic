### Findings

1. **crates/native/src/main.rs:196-200 (`observe_window_bounds` callback) / crates/native/src/strip.rs:83-97**: the strip's offset is clamped to `max_offset(width)` only inside `scroll_to`, which runs on a wheel or a page, never on a resize. When the strip is scrolled to its end (offset = `len * CELL_PITCH - width`) and the window then grows wider, `max_offset` shrinks but `offset` keeps its old value, so the last cell ends short of the right edge and a blank band as wide as the growth stays on the right until the next wheel or keypress (the DOM strip in the Tauri app re-clamps `scrollLeft` by itself, so this differs from it). The same applies when the window grows wider than the whole content (offset should drop to 0). Fix: add a pure `Strip::clamp(width) -> bool` (or `resize`) that calls `scroll_to(self.offset, width)`, call it in the bounds observer before `strip_moved`, and cover it with a unit test (scroll to the end at width 500, clamp at width 800, expect `offset == len * CELL_PITCH - 800`).
