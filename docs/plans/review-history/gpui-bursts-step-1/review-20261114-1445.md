### Findings

1. **crates/native/src/main.rs:4583-4591 (`paint_strip`'s band loop) / 4659-4661 (the rejected dim)**: The band does not follow the cell's own layers the way `.cell.burst::before` does. In Tauri, `.cell.rejected { opacity: 0.45 }` applies to the whole cell, including its `::before`. So a rejected member's whole band, including the 4 px above and below and the 8 px reach across the gap, is drawn at 0.45 of 0.08. In the native strip, `REJECTED_DIM` covers only `cell_bounds`, so those out-of-cell parts of a rejected member's band stay at full 0.08. The band visibly changes brightness at a rejected cell in Tauri but not here. There is a similar problem on selected cells. Because of `isolation: isolate`, Tauri paints the `z-index: -1` band above the cell's own background, so a selected member shows the 8% tint over `--muted`. The native code paints the band first, and then the opaque `MUTED_FILL` covers it inside the cell. The plan asks for "the Tauri geometry and colors", and `native-app.md` lists neither difference. Suggested fix: when `cell.marks.dimmed()`, paint that cell's band at the dimmed alpha (about `0xffffff09`, 0.08 × 0.45). For a selected cell, paint its in-cell part again over the fill, or paint each cell's band right after its selected fill (the bands only meet the neighbour's outer edge across the gap; they never overlap it). Alternatively, record both as deliberate differences in the `native-app.md` burst section.
2. **crates/native/src/main.rs:2434-2435 (`set_entries` doc comment)**: The comment still reads "Keep the folder's judgments, the view's own for a file judged since the open, and its facts", but the function now also regroups `bursts` over `all`. `native-app.md` says regrouping happens only here, so name it in the comment, e.g. "...and its facts, and regroup the bursts over `all`".
