STATUS: APPROVED

`open_main` takes the window setup out of `main` without changing it, apart from a documented reordering of `set_app_identity` and `on_window_closed` ahead of `gpui_kit::init`, which neither depends on. The `#[cfg(test)]` `Ui` helper keeps its index and settings store in a temporary folder, waits only within a time limit and checks that teardown is clean. The deviation from the plan (a `gpui-pre-platform` dev-dependency instead of `gpui-kit/test-support`) is explained in `docs/agents/native-app.md`, the learnings and `CLAUDE.md`.
