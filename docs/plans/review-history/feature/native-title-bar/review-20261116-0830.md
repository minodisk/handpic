## Round 1

**Reviewed commit**: `3a7604a9151ce35887470ffb24dac13631bfc6eb` feat(native): draw gpui-component's TitleBar in place of the OS title bar

STATUS: NEEDS_FIX

### Findings

1. **[crates/native/src/main.rs:5411 (with :595, :754, :4367)]**: Requesting `WindowDecorations::Client` makes `Root`'s `window_border()` pad the content on Linux by the client inset plus a 1 px border on every untiled side (gpui-component 0.9.1 `window_border.rs`: `SHADOW_SIZE` is 20 px on Linux, so about 21 px a side). Before this PR, Linux used server-side decorations and those paddings were 0. The view still measures from the whole viewport. `strip_width = window.viewport_size().width` (set at :754 and in the bounds observer at :595, then used by `scroll_into_view`, `visible_range`, `release_outside`, `next_to_request`, `clamp` and `carried_offset`) is now about 42 px wider than the strip really is. So on a floating Linux window, `scroll_into_view` can leave the current cell partly hidden past the right edge, and the range and clamp are off by that much. The new `menu_max` at :4367 is overstated by the vertical paddings in the same way. Suggested fix: subtract `gpui_component::window_paddings(window)` (public, and zero under server decorations and on Windows/macOS, where `SHADOW_SIZE` is 0) from the viewport in all three places: left + right for `strip_width`, top + bottom for `menu_max`. Note it in the new `docs/agents/native-app.md` section's Linux decorations rule. The alternative is to drop the explicit `window_decorations`, but the plan records that as the user's decision against it.

### Dismissed

None. Item 1 was fixed.

## Round 2

**Reviewed commit**: `65b2ce988a5b4cdc95b934ff9d1fdf3e4b12859f` fix: address local review round 1 findings

STATUS: NEEDS_FIX

**Previous feedback**: addressed 1 / dismissal accepted 0 / re-raised 0

### Findings

1. **[crates/native/src/main.rs:4499, :4510]**: This is the same problem as Round 1 item 1, but in code that fix did not touch. The strip's left and right mouse-down handlers pass the window-absolute `e.position.x` to `strip_context` / `strip_clicked`, which give it to `Strip::hit_test` as an offset from the strip's left edge. The comment says "The strip spans the window from its left edge." That stops being true on a floating Linux window: with `WindowDecorations::Client`, `Root`'s `window_border()` now insets the content by `window_paddings(window).left` (about 21 px). `paint_strip` draws from `bounds.origin`, so the cells move right by that much, but hit testing does not. A click near a cell's left edge selects the previous cell, and the gaps are misplaced. Suggested fix: subtract `gpui_component::window_paddings(window).left` from `e.position.x` in both handlers (the listener closures already receive `window`), and update the two comments to match. Another option is to take the x relative to the strip div's bounds.
2. **[crates/native/src/main.rs:5308-5316]**: The new `content_width` was inserted between `window_geometry`'s doc comment and its `fn`. Now `content_width` carries both texts ("The window's restore bounds and display; ..." followed by its own), and `window_geometry` has no doc comment. Move `content_width` and its comment above the `/// The window's restore bounds ...` lines, so each function keeps its own doc comment.

### Dismissed

None. Items 1 and 2 were fixed.
