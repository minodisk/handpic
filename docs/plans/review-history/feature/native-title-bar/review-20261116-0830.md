### Findings

1. **[crates/native/src/main.rs:5411 (with :595, :754, :4367)]**: Requesting `WindowDecorations::Client` makes `Root`'s `window_border()` pad the content on Linux by the client inset plus a 1 px border on every untiled side (gpui-component 0.9.1 `window_border.rs`: `SHADOW_SIZE` is 20 px on Linux, so about 21 px a side). Before this PR, Linux used server-side decorations and those paddings were 0. The view still measures from the whole viewport. `strip_width = window.viewport_size().width` (set at :754 and in the bounds observer at :595, then used by `scroll_into_view`, `visible_range`, `release_outside`, `next_to_request`, `clamp` and `carried_offset`) is now about 42 px wider than the strip really is. So on a floating Linux window, `scroll_into_view` can leave the current cell partly hidden past the right edge, and the range and clamp are off by that much. The new `menu_max` at :4367 is overstated by the vertical paddings in the same way. Suggested fix: subtract `gpui_component::window_paddings(window)` (public, and zero under server decorations and on Windows/macOS, where `SHADOW_SIZE` is 0) from the viewport in all three places: left + right for `strip_width`, top + bottom for `menu_max`. Note it in the new `docs/agents/native-app.md` section's Linux decorations rule. The alternative is to drop the explicit `window_decorations`, but the plan records that as the user's decision against it.

### Dismissed

None. Item 1 was fixed.
