## Round 1

**Reviewed commit**: `714f5fcc43baf24a5f27fc6c28039450d070b36f` feat(native): restore the window geometry and the last folder on launch

STATUS: NEEDS_FIX

### Findings

1. **crates/native/src/session.rs:48-57 (`Rect::within`)**: `f32::clamp` panics when `min > max`. The upper bound is `area.x + area.width - width`. When the saved window is at least as wide (or tall) as the visible area, `width == area.width`, and `(area.x + area.width) - area.width` is not exactly `area.x` in f32 when `area.x` has a fractional part: the sum rounds to the coarser ulp of the larger magnitude. For example, `x = 79.96`, `w = 1365.6` gives a bound of `79.95996 < 79.96`. A quick scan of fractional origins (multiples of 0.04) against four typical logical widths found 3840 such cases. Fractional visible origins are realistic on Windows. They come from a taskbar docked at the left or top at 125 % / 150 % scale (e.g. 48 px / 1.25 = 38.4) or from a secondary display's logical origin. That case is the one the guide lists as "Not seen yet". `place` runs in `main()` before the window opens, so this panic would happen on every launch for as long as that `windowBounds` stays in the store, and the app could not start. Fix: compute the bound without relying on exact cancellation, e.g. `let max_x = (area.x + area.width - width).max(area.x); x: self.x.min(max_x).max(area.x)` (same for y), or avoid `clamp`. Add a unit test with a fractional visible origin and a window wider and taller than the visible area (e.g. visible `rect(79.96, 38.4, 1365.6, 800.)`, saved `rect(0., 0., 4000., 3000.)`) that asserts no panic and a result inside the area.

### Fixed

1. `Rect::within` no longer uses `clamp`; the upper bound is floored at the area's origin. Test added for a fractional origin with an oversized window.
