1. **[crates/backend/src/update.rs:1260]**: This will fail on the macOS leg. `codesign_checks_the_staged_bundle_before_the_swap` asserts `exe_of(&bundle) == std::fs::read("/usr/bin/true")`, but `signed_bundle` runs `codesign --force --sign -` on the copy. That rewrites the copy's Mach-O in place: Apple's platform signature is replaced by an ad hoc one in every slice of the universal binary. So the swapped-in executable is no longer byte-identical to `/usr/bin/true`, and the assertion fails even when the swap is correct. Fix: capture the signed executable's bytes before building the archive and compare against those. For example, `let signed_exe = std::fs::read(app.join("Contents/MacOS/handpic")).unwrap();` right after `codesign_check(&app).unwrap();`, then `assert_eq!(exe_of(&bundle), signed_exe);`. The tampering only touches `Info.plist`, so reading it later would also work, but capturing it next to `good` makes the intent clear. (Round 1 missed this.)
